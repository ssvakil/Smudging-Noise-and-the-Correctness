==============================================================================
NOTE ON RNS-BASED DEPLOYMENTS
==============================================================================
Real production BFV deployments (SEAL, OpenFHE) typically use an RNS
modulus chain (several ~30-60 bit primes) rather than one single large
modulus. This paper's single-modulus model is a simplification stated
explicitly in Sec. 4 of the paper. Re-running this analysis under an
RNS chain is a natural next step and may change the numeric constants
(though we expect the qualitative conclusion -- that n_max is far below
realistic deployment cardinalities -- to persist, since RNS chains do
not fundamentally change the noise-vs-modulus relationship, only how
q is represented computationally).
